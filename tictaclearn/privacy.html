TicTacLearn: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

TicTacLearn does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account. You don't sign in, and the app does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and no third-party software that collects data.
  • No network use. The app does not connect to the internet. Training, playing and all lessons run entirely on your device.
  • What stays on your device: your settings, your lesson progress, and the AIs you train. These are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, and are deleted when you delete the app. You can also delete an AI you trained from the Train tab. Like any app's data, they can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups.
  • Links. The Sources and About screens contain links to public websites (for example research papers and this app's support page). Tapping one opens it in your web browser, or an email link in your email app, outside this app. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
